# MAYODE Website Design System

## Visual World

**Quiet cooperative software** presents MAYODE GROUP like a premium product company: generous white space, near-black typography, restrained blue actions, and real field imagery. The agricultural context comes from the content and photography; the interface stays calm, precise, and product-led.

## Typography

- `Outfit` carries headings, operational labels, interface text, and body copy: large, compact, and decisive.
- Editorial emphasis uses a restrained Georgia italic in headlines only.
- Headings are close-set and short; never use gradient text or decorative type effects.

## Color and Materials

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Near-black: `#1D1D1F`
- Secondary text: `#6E6E73`
- Action blue: `#0071E3`
- Soft surface: `#F5F5F7`

Use restrained photographic rice-field imagery, soft-gray surfaces, thin rules, and quiet rounded product panels. Shadows are rare and soft; surfaces remain mostly flat and purposeful.

## Composition

- Lead with a split editorial hero: farmer-facing language on paper beside real field photography.
- Use full-width dark-green operational bands for step-based journeys and section transitions.
- Reserve structured boards for real product truth: status, sequence, and next action — never decorative dashboard metrics.
- Let content move between wide editorial breathing room and compact record-like density.
- On small screens, preserve the reading order and reveal the field image as its own full-width moment.
- Product cards use a dense two-column grid with no dead cells; the product-family heading acts as a scroll-paced anchor while cards reveal into view.

## Components and Motion

- Calls to action are compact, square-cornered action labels rather than pills.
- Icons use the existing Hugeicons set in a consistent stroke weight.
- Accordions expose factual answers inline.
- Hover motion is limited to a small upward lift on action controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- GSAP handles the authored entrance, card reveal, pinned product rhythm, and image scale/fade scroll behavior. Respect reduced-motion preferences in the CSS fallback.

## Voice

Clear, grounded, and accountable. Speak about farmers, farms, AMCOS, and verification as real work; avoid inflated claims, fabricated outcomes, or fear-based urgency.
